Our story

Built in Hong Kong for buyers abroad.

DIKUROOMS Trading Co., Limited was set up to give European and American buyers a single desk for Asian trade — without losing the kind of patient, careful work that small trading companies are known for.

We operate from a small office in To Kwa Wan, Kowloon. We focus on five product categories (electronics, hardware fittings, home goods, general merchandise, packaging materials), import/export of goods and technology, and a growing practice in supply-chain and business-information consulting.

We believe that reliable trade is built on documents that travel well. Every order has a paper trail; every plan has a deadline. Long-term buyers know us for the daily calls and the emails that land on time.

What we believe

Four values, in plain English.

The rules of thumb we hold each other to — at the desk, on the dock, and on the phone.

Reliability

We say when we can deliver, then we do. If we slip, we tell you before you have to ask.

Transparency

Costs, lead times, and risks — written down, with the reasoning attached. No black boxes.

Craft

A buyer is a person, not a SKU. Each order gets the attention of the team doing the work.

Long-term thinking

We turn down the short wins that cost us the next five years. The relationship is the asset.
